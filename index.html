How the Platform Works

Engineered to replace fragmented WhatsApp groups with an intelligent medical dispatch pipeline.

1. Smart Donor Match Score

Transparent compatibility rating calculated by blood group compatibility (40%), proximity distance (25%), 1-tap availability (20%), and past response history (15%).

2. Emergency Notification Cascade

Staged notification expansion (5km to 10km to 25km to Regional Blood Banks) that prevents alert fatigue and ensures rapid responders receive priority.

3. Privacy-First Geolocation

Exact donor home GPS coordinates are never made public. Only approximate sector distances are computed to safeguard donor privacy.
